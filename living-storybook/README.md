# Chris — The Living Watercolor Storybook

10 looping clips (7 s, 24 fps, 876–888×1500 portrait, H.264, no audio) made from the supplied 10-panel watercolor sheet.

Every frame is the original painting, gently warped. Nothing is repainted. Wind ripples move through the scarf and hair, and the body breathes, leans and tilts with small rotations. The stars shimmer, the fox tilts its head, swishes its tail and blinks, the leaves and petals drift, the sand wisps blow and the clouds move.

Regenerate: `cd source && python3 scenes.py ../clips` (needs numpy, opencv-python-headless and ffmpeg).
