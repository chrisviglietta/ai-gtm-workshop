# Daily Morning Message to Alexa (mi amor 💛)

A recurring morning ritual: Chris sends Alexa a message with his day's agenda,
a short quote about being alive, and a personal thought/reflection.

## Process (tools + steps)

1. **Pull today's calendar** — Google Calendar MCP: `list_events` on the primary
   calendar (`chris.viglietta@clay.com`, timezone America/Los_Angeles), with
   `startTime`/`endTime` spanning today.
2. **Turn events into a simple bulleted agenda** — girlfriend-friendly, not work-jargon:
   - lowercase, casual labels ("chat w louis", not full meeting titles)
   - time first (e.g. `9am clay workshop`), skip prep/transit/manifest blocks
   - keep fun emoji where natural (🌮 lunch, 💃 salsa)
3. **Pick a short quote about being alive** — Mary Oliver is the default poet.
   Used so far: "Listen — are you breathing just a little, and calling it a life?"
   (from *Have You Ever Tried to Enter the Long Black Branches*). Rotate quotes
   so it doesn't repeat.
4. **Ask Chris for (or include) his morning thought** — a personal reflection,
   placed at the very end, lightly polished but kept in his voice (lowercase, informal).
5. **Deliver as a draft in chat** for Chris to copy and send — do not send it anywhere.

## Message format

```
hola mi amor 💛

"<short quote about being alive>" — Mary Oliver

here's what my day looks like today:

<time> <activity>
<time> <activity>
...

one last thing — a thought from this morning: <Chris's reflection, in his voice>
```
