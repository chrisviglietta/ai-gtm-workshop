FAQ

Common questions

What file should I send?

Vector files (AI, PDF, SVG) for anything we cut or engrave. High-resolution PDF or TIFF for printing. If you only have a JPG or a sketch, send it anyway and we'll tell you what we need.

Can you print white ink?

Yes. White ink goes under color on clear or dark materials so the colors read correctly. Put white on its own layer, or ask us to generate it.

What's the smallest order?

One piece for prototypes. Production minimums depend on the item. Publish minimums

Can I send my own items to engrave or print?

Yes. We'll test one before running the batch. Send a spare in case the test needs a second try.

Do you offer proofs?

Every job gets a digital proof. Physical samples are available before production runs.

Can you ship directly to my client without your branding?

Yes. Trade accounts can request blind, white-label shipping.

How do I mark cut lines for print and cut?

Use a spot color named CutContour (100% magenta), as a hairline stroke on its own layer.

Files ready?

Upload them with your quote request. Large files are fine.
