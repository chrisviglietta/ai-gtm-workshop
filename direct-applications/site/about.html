A Los Angeles shop that gets the job done on time.

Direct Applications is a production shop for printed, cut, and engraved pieces. We run our own equipment, and our operators have about ten years of production experience between the machines.

Shot list · Wide shot of the shop floor, machines running, team at work
The shop

How we work

Most of our customers are agencies, packaging firms, and brands that need a dependable production partner. They bring the artwork; we figure out how to make it, make a sample, and run the quantity.

Because printing, cutting, and engraving happen in the same building, a job doesn't wait on a second vendor. That's how we keep short runs fast and rush jobs possible.
