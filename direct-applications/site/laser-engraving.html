Laser and Rotary Engraving, Flat or Round

Our Epilog engraver marks names, logos, and artwork into the surface of a piece. The rotary attachment turns bottles, glasses, and tumblers under the laser, so the engraving wraps around the curve instead of sitting on one flat spot.
